# Organization settings and closing quarters

## Settings

Read every section from `GET /workspace` → `settings` (sections never saved come with their defaults). Save one with the command `{ "type": "saveSettings", "section": "…", "data": {…} }`: it **replaces the whole section**, so read it, change what you need and send it all back. Invalid data → `400 invalid_settings`. Settings change how every future document is numbered and printed: confirm with the user before saving.

### Invoice series

Every organization has two built-in series: `normal` (`AAAA-####`) and `rectifying` (`R-AAAA-####`). `invoiceSettings.series` holds the custom ones and any built-in one that was changed: `[{ id, type, name, mask, active? }]`. In a mask, `AAAA` (or `AA`) is the year and a run of `#` the counter, as wide as it is: `AAAA-B-####` → `2026-B-0001`. Built-in series keep their id and type; no two series may share a format; an inactive series takes no new invoices. A series that already numbers invoices or drafts can be renamed or deactivated, but not removed or re-masked (`409 series_in_use`). Documents name a custom series with `seriesId`.

## Closing quarters

Closing a quarter freezes its books (see records.md › Closed quarters). It is meant to happen when the user files forms 303 and 130, and the web app is what computes those: **don't close or reopen quarters unless the user explicitly asks**, and then with the figures they filed.

- `{ "type": "closeQuarter", "period": "2026-T3", "closedOn": "2026-10-20", "returns": [...] }`. Closing a closed quarter again updates its date.
- `returns` (optional, up to 10): the returns as filed, `{ id, form, period, boxes, result, filedOn }`. `form` is `303` or `130`, once each; `period` must be the quarter being closed; `boxes` maps box numbers (`"07"`, `"110"`) to values (cents, or a percentage for rate boxes); `result` in cents, positive to pay, negative to offset or refund. They are never changed: closing again after reopening stores a new version, and the latest counts. Later returns start from them.
- `{ "type": "reopenQuarter", "period": "2026-T3" }`. A quarter that isn't closed → `404`.

Read them from `GET /workspace`: `closedQuarters` (`{ period, closedOn }`) and `filedReturns`.
