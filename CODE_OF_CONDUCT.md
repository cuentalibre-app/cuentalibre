# Code of conduct

cuentalibre is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that maintainers have the last word on what goes into the project.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, write to the maintainers at the contact address in the [aviso legal](https://cuentalibre.es/aviso-legal). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules, temporarily or for good, depending on how serious and repeated it is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
